title: "Compile-time function evaluation"
category: news
summary: "Added initial CTFE"
---

Datalove has a restrictive type system
that enables lots of fun features.

Because functions are pure,
deterministic with no side effects,
and we have a working interpreter,
and no pointers,
it's almost trivial to evaluate
functions at compile time.

Getting this feature in early
to inform the architucture.
Initial feature const-evals
all `const` statements,
in scripts, script functions,
modules, and module functions.
After that works fully
I'll try to add Zig-like comptime function arguments.

The simple specialization that requires
will hopefully inform full genereics.
